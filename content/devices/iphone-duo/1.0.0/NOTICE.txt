This resource replaces the earlier ios27-duo-kit procedural approximation.
The model geometry, skinning animation, material parameters, textures, reflector
cards, and HDR environments originate from the referenced Apple Lotus scene.
These assets are not covered by the Apache-2.0 license of ios27-duo-kit.
Original asset rights remain with their respective owners.

ScreenCam conversion: native geometry with five-degree fold samples, rigid-node
transforms, PNG material maps, and linear half-float radiance cubes generated from
the authored reflector-card configuration (PT_Landscape) and HDR environments.
ACESFilmic tone mapping and per-material environment layers/exposure are retained.
Recorded screen pixels replace the original wallpapers and UI at render time.
See source-config.json for original scene configuration and source SHA-256 hashes.
